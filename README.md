👋 Hi, I’m Anthony.

I enjoy working on IoT projects for my camper van and building Home Assistant integrations for home energy and utilities.

# Projects

🚐 Van Automation (ESPHome):
- [Ford Transit Door Interface](https://github.com/anthonysecco/ford-transit-door-interface): monitor and control the van's doors
- [Thermostat for Undermount AC](https://anthonysecco.github.io/undermount-ac-esphome-thermostat/)
- [Thermostat for Rixen MCS6](https://github.com/anthonysecco/rixen-mcs6-esphome-thermostat): PID fan control and an enhanced idle mode
- [Victron BLE Monitoring](https://github.com/anthonysecco/victron-orion-tr-home-assistant): Orion-Tr DC-DC charger data

🚙 Vehicle Data (CANbus):
- [WiCAN Ford Transit](https://github.com/anthonysecco/WiCAN-Ford-Transit): connect a Ford Transit to Home Assistant

📡 Networking & Connectivity:
- [OpenModem](https://github.com/anthonysecco/OpenModem): lightweight local web UI for the Quectel RM520N-GL modem
- [OpenModem for Home Assistant](https://github.com/anthonysecco/openmodem-ha): HACS integration for OpenModem
- [RPi5 Wi-Fi 6 AP](https://github.com/anthonysecco/rpi5-wifi-6-ap): Raspberry Pi 5 Wi-Fi 6 access point

⚡ Energy & Utilities (Home Assistant):
- [CAISO Stats](https://github.com/anthonysecco/caiso-stats): California grid status, demand, supply mix and load-shifting signals
- [WattTime Stats](https://github.com/anthonysecco/watttime-stats): live grid emissions, forecasts and clean-energy windows
- [San Jose Water Stats](https://github.com/anthonysecco/sjwater-stats): hourly water usage plus leak and high-use alerts

🏕️ Travel & Location (Home Assistant):
- [Public Lands](https://github.com/anthonysecco/public-lands-ha-sensor): identify the public land you're parked on
